Plastic Surgery Overview

Plastic surgery is a specialized surgical field that focuses on restoring, reconstructing, or altering the human body. Whether for aesthetic enhancement or reconstructive purposes, our board-certified plastic surgeons deliver natural-looking results with the highest safety standards.

At Yuva Skin Clinic, we combine artistic vision with surgical precision to help you achieve your aesthetic goals. Our state-of-the-art facility and experienced team ensure you receive the best possible care throughout your journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

All procedures are performed under anesthesia, so you won't feel pain during surgery. Post-operative discomfort varies by procedure but is well-managed with medication. Most patients report manageable pain that subsides within the first week.

Recovery varies by procedure. Minimally invasive procedures: 1-2 weeks. Major surgeries: 4-6 weeks for full recovery. Most patients return to work within 1-3 weeks depending on their job nature.

All surgeries leave some scars, but our surgeons use advanced techniques to minimize visibility. Incisions are placed in natural creases or hidden areas. Scars typically fade significantly over 12-18 months.

Results are long-lasting but not immune to aging. Breast augmentation: 10-15 years. Facelift: 10+ years. Liposuction permanently removes fat cells. Maintaining stable weight helps preserve results.

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans and financing options through our partner providers. Our patient coordinators can discuss available options during your consultation.

Important Information

Not a cosmetic procedure: Plastic surgery is a serious surgical procedure requiring proper evaluation. Realistic expectations are essential for satisfaction.

Results depend on: Individual healing, surgeon skill, and adherence to post-op instructions.
